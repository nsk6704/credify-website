Why "Credify"?

The name comes from "credits" - every positive action you take earns you XP (experience points). It's about gamifying your journey to better living. Like earning credits in a game, each workout, expense tracked, or journal entry brings you closer to leveling up in real life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why install from unknown sources?

Credify is completely open source—every line of code is public on GitHub. The APK distribution keeps the app 100% free with no ads or tracking, unlike Play Store alternatives.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely! All data is stored locally on your device. Credify never sends information to external servers. You have complete control—export or delete anytime.

Works offline?

Yes! Credify works completely offline. All features are available without an internet connection since everything is stored locally.
