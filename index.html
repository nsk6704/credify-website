About Credify

Credify is an open-source personal growth tracking application that gamifies your journey to self-improvement. By combining habit tracking with game mechanics like XP, levels, and streaks, Credify makes staying consistent with your goals more engaging and rewarding.

Built with modern technologies including React Native, TypeScript, and Expo, Credify delivers a native mobile experience while keeping your data private and secure on your device.
